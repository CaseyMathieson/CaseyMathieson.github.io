Calibration workflow · 2024–2025

Calibration workflow

Built a repeatable import, validation, comparison, and controlled-export process for field calibration updates.

Problem

Updates arrived in multiple legacy and current formats. Manual entry and scattered checks made a full run take about 14 elapsed hours with two people and always required remote support.

What I built

  • A worksheet-backed workflow with macro-enabled buttons for importing and formatting records, eliminating manual re-entry.
  • Format-aware import, before/after comparison, validation, one pass/fail result, and operator-facing error messages.
  • A documented procedure and field-service training; both trainees later completed the process independently.

Result

~14 hrsprevious elapsed duration · two people
2–3 hrsupdated elapsed duration · one person
2 engineerstrained on the documented workflow

Built-in safeguards

  • Validate before export: importing data never made it ready for use by itself.
  • Show exceptions clearly: missing or incompatible inputs became a specific correction task.
  • Design for handoff: the procedure and training made the workflow repeatable beyond its original developer.
